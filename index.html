The Shogunate 将軍

Ranked by Elo rating, the same system used for chess. Everyone starts at 1500. After each game, every player is compared with every other player at the table. Beating someone rated higher earns more points than beating someone rated lower. Rising Sun is a long game, so it takes 2 battles to earn a rank. Warlords with only 1 battle are Ronin (unranked).

Quick Rules 心得

The short version, for when it's been a while.

誉
Golden rule: every tie goes to higher Honor.Battles, Kami shrines, Harvest rewards, war bids, who acts first when abilities collide, and the final score. The only exception is allies tied for first at game end, who share the win.

⚠ Often forgotten

1 Tea Ceremony

  • Alliances are pairs and last this season only, unless someone Betrays.
  • House rule You can't ally with the same person more than once a game, unless no one else will ally with you.
  • Bribes (Coins, Ronin) are allowed now and in the Political Phase, never during War. Deals aren't binding.

2 Political Phase

  • Mandate bonus goes to the chooser and their ally. Everyone else gets the basic effect.
  • The chooser goes last, except on Train, where the chooser buys first.
  • Betray breaks your alliance and costs Honor (no Honor loss if you had no ally). It swaps figures, it doesn't kill them. You can't swap a Daimyo or a Shinto on a shrine.
  • Shinto only reach a shrine when Summoned, then stay there all season.
  • Max 4 Strongholds.

3 War Phase

  • Only you and your ally in a province? No battle. The most Force takes it.
  • Only one clan in a province? They take it, no battle.
  • You, your ally and anyone else? Everyone fights, allies included. If your ally wins, your figures there survive.
  • Hire Ronin doesn't spend your Ronin. Winning it with none still blocks everyone else.
  • Winner pays the losers: the winner splits their bid Coins among the losers. Losers' bids go to the bank.
  • Hostages come back at the start of next season. A Daimyo can't be taken.

End of season: unspent Coins and Ronin go back to the bank. Spend them or lose them.

A season (×3: Spring, Summer, Autumn)

  1. Setup: war province tokens out, take income.
  2. Tea Ceremony: form alliances (pairs, this season only).
  3. Political: 3 Mandates → Kami · 2 Mandates → Kami · 2 Mandates → Kami.
  4. War: provinces fight in war-number order.
  5. Cleanup: Coins & Ronin to the bank, Shinto come home.

Mandate turn: draw 4, keep 1 secretly, put 3 back on top.

War, step by step

Everyone bids Coins secretly on the steps, then reveal. Highest bid wins each step (Honor breaks ties).

  1. Seppuku: kill all your figures here for 1 VP + 1 Honor each.
  2. Take Hostage: grab 1 non-Daimyo figure and steal 1 VP.
  3. Hire Ronin: each of your Ronin is +1 Force.
  4. Battle: most Force wins the province. Losers' figures die (unless allied to the winner).
  5. Imperial Poets: 1 VP per figure killed in this battle.

Kami favors

Most Force on a shrine during a Kami turn gets it. Resolve shrines left to right.

    Scoring

    • War provinces won: Spring 1 VP, Summer 2, Autumn 3 each.
    • Different provinces collected: 3–4 = 10 VP · 5–6 = 20 · 7–8 = 30.
    • Plus Winter-upgrade Season cards.
    • Most VP wins. Tie → higher Honor, unless the tied players are allies: then they share the win.
